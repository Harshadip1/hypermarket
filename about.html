Our Story & Commitment in Akola

Open Challenge is Akola's favorite one-stop supermarket chain, offering shoppers a comprehensive range of home and personal products under one roof.

Established with a commitment to offer unbeatable savings to the citizens of Akola, Open Challenge operates 4 major retail branches across Akola: Murtizapur Road, Civil Lines, Jowahar Nagar, and Old City Market.

Each Open Challenge store in Akola stocks complete home utility products - including daily groceries, edible oils, Atta, rice, personal care items, garments, kitchenware, and bed linen - all priced lower than MRP every day.
